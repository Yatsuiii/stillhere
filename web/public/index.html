6. Film: the same loop on video

Why this station: the face test in Evidence asks how TRIBE's forecast changes when the faces in a film are filled in. SAM 3.1 masked the faces in the first 190 s of Tears of Steel; StillHere filled them with the surrounding colour, and filled a same-size patch elsewhere in each frame as a control. TRIBE forecasts all three versions, and the face test compares the forecast's face map with face maps from the scans of 16 people who watched the film. Type "face", or another word, to make the same edit on three frames here.
