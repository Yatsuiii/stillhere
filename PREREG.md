# StillHere pre-registration (v3, frozen at the SHA-256 recorded in STATUS.md and the submission)

Written Oct 22 2026 (v2), revised Oct 23 2026 (v3) from PLAN §5 (cloud thread, Oct 22 update) plus the local development-set work on public Le Petit Prince (LPP) data. Freeze target: Sun Oct 25 2026, before any TRIBE-vs-ds003171 score exists. At freeze: compute the SHA-256 of this file, post it in the project thread, record it in STATUS.md and in the submission. Nothing in ds003171 has been scored against TRIBE at the time of drafting.

**Confidentiality of this file.** This document is public. It holds nothing derived from the sedation clip: no clip audio, words, timings, word counts, predictions, separability numbers, ranks, or projected scores. Where a rule depends on a clip-derived quantity (clip duration, ASR word count, separability), the quantity is measured inside the private run and reported with the results; it is not stated here.

**Terminology.** The gauge formerly called UNDERSTANDING is called **LANGUAGE**: a language-region response to speech, locked to the real word sequence if P8 passes. The word "understanding" is not used for any result.

## 1. Data and unit of analysis

- ds003171: 17 subjects x 4 clip runs (Awake, Light, Deep, Recovery; 155 volumes, TR 2 s) + 4 rest runs (256 volumes). Run mapping by sidecar `AcquisitionTime`, not filename. Known exceptions: story-after-rest in sub-08BC Light and sub-17EK Recovery; sub-10JR names differ (Deep status uncertain; sensitivity analysis without 10JR-Deep).
- The subject is the unit for every group statistic.
- Scored volumes j in [10, j_max], j_max = min(154, floor((T_clip - 1 + 5 + L - 1.92)/2)), T_clip = the clip's duration from the authors' file (not reproduced here).
- Language beat and calibration: LPP section 1, English (49), French (28; primary for hardware similarity), Chinese exploratory. These are DEVELOPMENT sets (see section 8); held-out English section 8 supplies the stage numbers (§8).

## 2. Models

- **Primary model rule (from `UPGRADE-TRIBE.md` step 4).** Stock TRIBE v2 (`facebook/tribev2`) is primary. Tuning never touches LPP-EN section 8 (held out for every stage number) or LPP section 1 (all languages, evaluation), and never ds003171; a tuned model whose training manifest includes section 8 is not eligible and every stage number then comes from stock TRIBE. Tuned TRIBE becomes primary if, on held-out LPP-EN section 1 listeners, its mean LANGUAGE z beats stock by >= 0.10 AND its HEARING z is not more than 0.10 lower. Tuning hold-out listeners (fixed here): per language, sorted listener IDs, `numpy.random.default_rng(seed).permutation`, the first third are held out of every section. English seed 20261025 (49 listeners, 16 held out): EN058, EN059, EN062, EN065, EN068, EN079, EN082, EN088, EN093, EN097, EN101, EN103, EN109, EN110, EN114, EN115. French seed 20261026 (28 listeners, 9 held out): FR001, FR005, FR006, FR014, FR016, FR022, FR023, FR024, FR025. Chinese is not used. The choice and the tuned weights' SHA-256 are fixed and posted before any ds003171 scoring. If tuning is not finished by Wed Oct 28 noon ET, stock is primary at Gate 1 and tuned is reported later as a pre-registered second model. Both are shown.
- Predictions: full, audio-only, text-only and shuffled-word, stitched from 50 s overlapping windows (the value from the window where each second is closest to the centre), aligned per vertex.
- **Alignment and the lag rule (replaces PLAN §3 step 5; from ANSWERS-2026-10-22b §1, measured on public LPP).** Vertex v of volume j gets P(2j + s_v - 5 - L), s_v from the sidecar SliceTiming map warped to the surface, no slice-timing correction; positive L means the BOLD is later than the model. **L = L0 + dL.**
  - **L0** = TRIBE's group-peak HEARING lag on LPP section 1, measured through this exact pipeline (raw middle echo, 24 motion regressors + aCompCor-5, per-vertex slice times, EN's 4 pre-onset scans dropped) on a 0.1 s grid with a parabolic peak and listener bootstrap. Provisional: **EN +0.89 s (95% CI +0.56, +1.31), n = 20**; **final (Oct 23, full raw runs, pictures masked for EN): EN L0 = +0.90 s, 95% CI [+0.42, +1.40], n = 49; FR +0.62 s [+0.35, +0.96], n = 28 (EN-FR difference 0.28 s). The CI half-widths (0.49 s EN, 0.31 s FR) are wider than the 0.25 s target; L0 = +0.90 s is frozen (the English value, not a pooled EN+FR one: the sedation clip is English and EN has the larger n; a pooled value differs by about 0.1 s, under 1% of the hearing r) and its uncertainty is carried, not hidden: a 0.5 s timing error keeps 94-95% of the peak HEARING r (EN, relative r at L0 -0.5/+0.5 s: 0.95/0.94; at -1/+1 s: 0.82/0.77).** No ds003171 data are used. (The LPP derivatives' ME-ICA step slice-time-corrected to the mean slice time, about 0.97 s, which is why TRIBE peaks near 0 on the derivatives and near +0.9 s on this clock.)
  - **dL = 0 unless the onset check fails.** On Awake runs that pass the motion rules, at the rule-chosen high-pass, before the headphone check, with no TRIBE: Lenv = parabolic argmax over [-14, +14] s of the group-mean Fisher-z r(HRF x envelope sampled at 2j + s_v - l, AUD BOLD). Lcal = Lenv - b, where b = (envelope peak) - (TRIBE peak) on the same LPP runs at that high-pass (raw clock, pictures masked, final: EN -1.26 s, FR -1.26 s, EN-FR difference 0.00 s; earlier unmasked EN -1.04 s; derivative clock across 0.01-0.03 Hz and 145-TR/full windows: EN -0.87 to -1.20 s, FR -1.36 to -1.45 s; EN-FR difference 0.38 s). If |Lcal - L0| >= **T s** with **T = 2.0 s** (frozen Oct 23 after check 6: a true 2 s shift triggers at 2.0 s in 48-63% of 1,000 draws of 17 listeners, below 80%, which first suggested 1.75 s; but the envelope lag drifts by 1.29 s between English sections 1 and 8 (peak -1.25 s vs -2.55 s, same estimator), which makes 1.75 s switch falsely in 3.8% of draws, over the 2% limit; at 2.0 s the false-switch rate with that drift is 0.2%, and with no drift 0%; Yatsu chose 2.0 s. At 2.0 s a true 4 s mismatch triggers in 100% of draws with no drift and with adverse drift (it then reads about 2.7 s), 6 s and 8 s in 100%; a true 2 s shift triggers in about half the draws with no drift and not at all with adverse drift, so the check is not claimed to catch 2 s shifts), the subject-bootstrap 95% CI of Lcal excludes L0, and the peak is a single clear maximum at least 2 s from the range edge, then dL = Lcal - L0; otherwise dL = 0.
  - **One L for every subject, state and gauge.** No per-run or per-state lags.
  - **Canonical-HRF regressors use their own timing, L + b:** the B2 envelope baseline, HRF x word-rate (plus its temporal derivative) inside LANGUAGE and PULA, the headphone check, and the word-statistics baselines. Giving them TRIBE's L would repeat the misalignment behind the withdrawn "three-fold" claim.
  - **L uses TRIBE on public LPP only, never with ds003171 BOLD.**
  - **Reported line (not a bar): hearing vs the classic sound test on ds003171.** At the frozen lags (TRIBE at L, the HRF x envelope baseline at L + b), the same scoring, window and nulls, reported as TRIBE HEARING z vs aligned envelope z with the paired difference. Reference from the held-out English LPP section 8, timing fixed in advance (dev lag -1.25 s): TRIBE 2.14, envelope 1.10 (paired p = 3e-6); at the section's own best lag the envelope was 1.51. The development section was a tie. The public wording stays "agrees with the classic sound test" whatever this line shows.
- **Fixed order on ds003171:** high-pass rule from rest data (Oct 27), then the onset check, then the headphone check, then scoring. No envelope-BOLD correlation on ds003171 before the freeze.
  - **Sensitivity (reported, never chosen):** Lcal, L +/- 0.5 s, L +/- 1 s, and Deep scored at L + (Deep - Awake envelope lag). If propofol delays the Deep envelope lag by 1 s or more (subject-bootstrap CI), say so; P1 is the bar most exposed to it. Costs measured on LPP: a 0.5 s error loses about 5-6% of the HEARING correlation, 1 s about 20%.
  - All regressors are built by one function (`timing.hrf_regressor`) with an explicit lag; its sign convention and a known-shift test (recovering +3.0, -2.0 and 0 s) are pre-freeze checks, and the earlier harness's regressors (a 0.25 s bias) are replaced.

## 3. Scores (harness frozen at the LPP-development defaults)

- **HEARING** = Fisher-z mean over the 7 AUD parcels (Schaefer-400/17, `SomMotB_Aud`) of r(parcel BOLD, parcel P_audio). Primary uses audio-only predictions. A secondary HEARING_full uses P_full (its adoption rule passed in EN (+0.25 z) and FR (+0.38 z) and was overridden a priori, before any ds003171 scoring: P_full carries the text pathway and blurs the HEARING/LANGUAGE split that P3 needs. It stays out of every bar; it is reported in every state and as a P3 sensitivity, with P3 also computed on an envelope-based HEARING (descriptive), and the Awake-to-Deep fall of HEARING_full minus HEARING is a descriptive TRIBE-only readout of language in auditory cortex. HEARING_full never overrides the P2a or P3 fallbacks).
- **LANGUAGE** = Fisher-z mean over the 19 LANG parcels (LH `TempPar_1-6`, `DefaultB_Temp_1-6`, `DefaultB_PFCv_1-5`, `DefaultB_PFCl_1-2`) of the partial correlation r(BOLD, P_full | P_audio, HRF x word-rate sampled at L + b, and its temporal derivative). (The word-rate timing fix was adopted as a bug fix; on LPP it left the numbers unchanged: EN real 1.75 to 1.73, shuffled 0.77 to 0.75; FR 1.12 to 1.11 and 0.09 to 0.09.) Word rate comes from the private transcript onsets. Parcel-level scoring; scored volumes 10..j_max (§1 above), up to 145 volumes (10..154); parcel means of mid-depth (0.5) ball projections onto fsaverage5.
- **Separability rule:** if 1 - r^2(P_full, P_audio) < 0.1 in LANG (measured from predictions only, inside the private run), LANGUAGE uses P_text in place of P_full. The outcome is reported with the results, not pre-stated.
- **Units:** z_rest = (score - rest-null mean) / rest-null SD, per subject and state, from that state's rest run. Never place these beside the z units used on LPP (windows against circular shifts).
- Secondary: delta r = r_full - r_audio; cross-validated R^2 gain; regression slope; FPN version of LANGUAGE; context ablation (`sentence_only=True`); HEARING_full.
- Variants tried on the development set and not adopted (reported, not used): band-limit with a 0.10 Hz low-pass, boxcar alignment, delta-prediction target, half-second lag, TRIBE-variance parcel weights, vertex-level scoring, 2-pass smoothing (AUD vertex-level 2.30 vs 2.18, language no gain), speech-TR selection (hurts). Rule for any future variant: chosen on one language, it must beat the default on the other (paired one-sided Wilcoxon p < 0.05 and +0.2 z) before use.

## 4. Fixed rules (none use story BOLD with TRIBE)

- **High-pass:** the lowest of {0.01, 0.015, 0.02, 0.03} Hz at which the N3 group mean (Awake rest x TRIBE prediction) is < 0.05 for both gauges. Decided Tue Oct 27 from rest data only. The ISC baseline uses the same filter and its own rest null. 0.02 Hz is a sensitivity analysis if not chosen.
- **Nuisance:** 24 motion regressors + aCompCor-5, detrend, high-pass, z-score. No global-signal regression (sensitivity analysis only), no smoothing.
- **Run exclusion:** mean FD > 0.5 mm, or > 30% of frames with FD > 0.5 mm; frames with FD > 0.5 mm are censored in scores and nulls alike.
- **Subject exclusion (headphone check, replaces PLAN's rule).** Exclude a subject only if BOTH the Awake envelope test (HRF x envelope at timing L + b against AUD BOLD, circular-shift one-sided p >= 0.05) AND the AUD LOO-ISC (Awake template excluding the subject, same null) miss p < 0.05. Reason: PLAN's single-test rule would drop 57% of genuine English and 39% of French LPP listeners at 145 TRs (limit 10%); this rule drops 4% (EN) and 7% (FR).
- **Effective-N floor:** Deep effective N below 10 makes the sedation results descriptive only (Gate 1 table).
- **Demo volunteer:** the included subject with the median Awake HEARING (rule, not by eye).
- **Fixed run order caveat:** Awake, Light, Deep, Recovery are always in that order; P6 bounds the repetition confound.

## 5. Nulls and baselines

- N1 circular shift (>= 15 TRs), reported only (too lenient). N2 phase-randomised text residual. **N3 (primary): same-state rest run**, scored against the clip predictions; per-subject SD from circular shifts of three rest crops starting at volumes 0, 55, 111. N4 wrong story (an LPP-EN section prediction). N5 visual parcels, descriptive.
- **Wrong-story false-positive check (from M3):** every method's wrong-story false-positive rate is reported next to its detection rate; detection percentages are shown only for cells at or below 7%.
- **B1:** LOO-ISC against an Awake template that excludes the scored subject (own rest null).
- **B2 (strongest practical no-volunteer reference):** HRF x envelope at timing L + b with one group-level lag (never a per-subject lag), compared with TRIBE on identical TRs (English pictures masked for both; windows starting at volume 10). On LPP at 145 TRs TRIBE hearing and the aligned envelope match: EN 1.47 vs 1.47 (HL envelope minus TRIBE +0.00, 95% CI -0.22 to +0.24), EN pictures masked 1.05 vs 1.07, FR 2.18 vs 1.78 (HL -0.36, CI -0.86 to +0.08). The word "matches" is used only if the HL CI of (envelope minus TRIBE) includes 0 or favours TRIBE in both languages; if the envelope wins in masked English, the comparison stays backstage. **Encompassing test (AUD):** partial r(BOLD, P_audio | aligned envelope) is above 0 (EN z 0.66, p = 4e-6; FR 1.46, p = 3e-6), so "more than acoustics" is supported; the envelope also adds beyond TRIBE. If TRIBE's partial r is not above 0 (group p < 0.05) on ds003171 Awake runs (English only), the "more than acoustics" sentence is struck. No claim that TRIBE beats the envelope for hearing.
- **B3:** word rate, partialled inside LANGUAGE (at timing L + b, plus its derivative). **B4 (language baselines):** word frequency, syntactic node counts where parses exist, and **LM surprisal** (GPT-2 small for English, a small French GPT-2 for French), all sampled at L + b with the same nuisance set, windows and nulls as LANGUAGE, and each also scored without partialling (a baseline that reads 0 there is a code-check). On LPP dev section 1 (145 TRs = 4.8 min): EN surprisal 0.96 vs TRIBE 1.73 (paired p = 8e-6, TRIBE beats it); FR surprisal 0.86 vs TRIBE 1.11 (p = 0.31, not shown). The language stage line is English-only (Yatsu, Oct 22): it needs TRIBE to beat the best baseline in English, confirmed on the held-out English section 8. Result (Oct 23, dev lag, 145 TRs): surprisal 0.68 vs TRIBE 1.76 (paired p = 2.4e-7), passed; French is reported as not shown.

## 6. Statistics and bars

Paired one-sided Wilcoxon, sign test, Hodges-Lehmann median difference with bootstrap CI. The headline needs all three co-primary bars (P1, P3, P8); no multiplicity correction for that conjunction. Holm correction over P2a, P2b, P4, P5, P6. P8 uses Awake runs only (Awake + Recovery is a secondary; Recovery is the fourth hearing of the clip). Power is computed from 145-volume crops of LPP run through the same pipeline (middle echo, no ME-ICA), never from clip-derived quantities; LPP measurements put the language effect at about z 1.75 (EN) per listener with the pipeline above, and the Awake-to-Deep power depends on how much of it survives deep sedation (simulated one-sided Wilcoxon, effect fully removed: >= 0.96 at n = 10-17; half remaining: 0.52-0.78 at n = 10-17).

| Bar | Pass if | If it fails, the claim becomes |
|---|---|---|
| **P1 (co-primary)** | LANGUAGE Awake > Deep, paired Wilcoxon p < 0.05, **and** Awake > Deep in at least k*(N) of N included subjects (k*: N=17:13, 16:12, 15:12, 14:11, 13:10, 12:10, 11:9, 10:9; smallest k with one-sided binomial p <= 0.05) | HEARING + ISC replication; "TRIBE-only LANGUAGE did not reach the bar" |
| **P3 (co-primary)** | HL median of (L_awake - L_deep) - (H_awake - H_deep) in z_rest units > 0, bootstrap CI excludes 0 | One combined gauge, no split |
| **P8 (co-primary)** | In Awake, LANGUAGE with the real prediction > with the shuffled-word prediction, paired Wilcoxon p < 0.05 | Name the gauge SPEECH-LOCKED; do not claim it follows the word sequence |
| P2a | HEARING in Deep above the rest null, group p < 0.05 | At most one global stimulus-tracking gauge |
| P2b | Individual detection rate: TRIBE's rate >= LOO-ISC's rate on the same runs - 15 points. **Expected to fail** (public LPP: EN hearing 41% vs ISC 94%, EN language 59% vs 88%; see the M3 caveat in section 7) | No individual-level claim |
| P4 | AUC for Awake vs Deep >= 0.80 for LANGUAGE and for L - H (HEARING AUC descriptive: it is hypothesised to survive), with subject-clustered bootstrap CIs, and delta AUC vs LOO-ISC on the same runs >= -0.05 (point estimates, no equivalence claim). Delta AUC is **likely to fail** [inference; LPP has no Deep state] | "Replaces controls at group level only" |
| P5 | Each of N3 and N4: group mean < 1/3 of the Awake story score (upper bootstrap CI of the ratio < 0.5), and story > null at p < 0.05; N5 descriptive | Limitation; downgrade the affected gauge |
| P6 | LANGUAGE Recovery > Deep, and median Recovery >= 0.5 x median Awake (z_rest) | Awake vs Deep only; state the repetition confound |
| P7 (pipeline sanity, first) | LOO-ISC in FPN Awake > Deep, and AUD ISC in Deep above its null | Fix preprocessing before reading anything else |

**Gate 1 decision table (Wed Oct 28, 9 PM ET):** P7 fails -> fix preprocessing (Thu buffer), read nothing else, still failing Thu 9 PM -> F2 (lead with LPP). Deep effective N below 10 -> sedation descriptive only, F2. P7 passes but HEARING in Awake not above the rest null -> F2 (overrides P5's downgrade). P1 fails -> HEARING + ISC replication. P3 fails -> one combined gauge. P8 fails -> SPEECH-LOCKED. P2a fails -> one global stimulus-tracking gauge. All co-primary bars pass -> full headline. **No shelving after Oct 26;** the only shelve point is Sun Oct 25, and only if audio-only TRIBE also fails on the clip in the private run.

## 8. Development-set discipline and the held-out section

LPP section 1 (EN and FR) is the development set: 40+ variants and diagnostics have been tried on it (listed in section 3 and in the project's result log). ## 9. Story-picker addendum (second beat; leaves PREREG if not frozen by Sat Oct 24 evening)

Stock TRIBE v2 only. Public data only; never ds003171, never the clip, never the private dataset.
- **Predictors** (TRIBE predictions plus each clip's own audio and words; no BOLD): **PULA** = mean over LANG parcels of sd_t(R), R = residual of P_full after OLS on [1, P_audio, HRF x word-rate] (raw TRIBE units, same harness as the gauges: stitching, 5 s offset, each dataset's TR grid, the parcel lists above, detrend and high-pass with the cutoff in Hz; music rows and the first 10 s dropped). **PH** = mean over AUD parcels of cov(P_full, P_audio)/sd(P_audio). Baselines TRIBE must beat: B-WR (words per second), B-ENV (sd of HRF x envelope after the gauge filter), B-SPF (speech fraction, descriptive). Content-free control PULA_timing (same onsets, each word's Llama vector replaced by the clip-mean vector), if the cache allows.
- **Targets (healthy public listeners, identical harness):** T1 mean over listeners of the stock-TRIBE LANGUAGE gauge; T2p model-free (20 random templates of k = 15 other listeners of the same clip, partial r with the template partialling HRF x envelope and HRF x word-rate); HEARING T3 = r(BOLD_AUD, P_audio), T4 = AUD ISC(15). Units for S4: z in non-overlapping 290 s windows against circular shifts. Ranks only within blocks (dataset x language x scanner); minimum 16 listeners per clip; first exposure only; split-half reliability reported as an upper bound.
- **Clips:** LPP-EN and LPP-FR sections 1-9 (section 1 included with a sensitivity run that drops it); Narratives stories (`notthefall` stays out); Chinese descriptive only. **Floor:** at least 30 clips in at least 3 blocks with at least 2 blocks outside LPP, else the result is a descriptive pilot with no headline wording. Counting by families. **Download deadline (Yatsu, Oct 23): the Narratives stories download only after Gate 1 (Wed Oct 28); if they have not landed and passed the 95% alignment rule by Thu Oct 29 12:00, the picker is reported as a next step with no bars scored.**
- **Bars (one-sided, 10,000 within-block permutations moving whole families, run once on a manifest hashed Thu Oct 29 noon ET, after Gate 1):** S1 (co-primary) pooled within-block Spearman rho(PULA, T1) > 0, p < 0.05; S3 (co-primary) partial rho(PULA, T1 | B-WR, B-ENV) > 0, p < 0.05; S3b partial on PULA_timing > 0; S2 (wording) rho(PULA, T2p) > 0, else say "readout" never "real brains"; S4 (impact) mean listener z290 of each block's top PULA tertile vs all clips in the block with a family-bootstrap CI above 0, also stated as listening time for equal z; descriptive: HEARING (PH vs T3, T4), within-clip window pairs, calibration slope, sensitivity runs.
