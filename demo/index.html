StillHere Pilot

Labs study people who can't respond after brain injury by playing them stories in the scanner. But every new story needs its own 12-15 healthy volunteers scanned first, in that language. For a new story, StillHere gives labs a first reading with zero volunteer scans, measured against each listener's own brain. It has been tested only in healthy volunteers.

1 in 4 brain-injured patients who could not follow commands at the bedside showed command-following brain activity on fMRI or EEG (Bodien et al., NEJM 2024).

6. Did the forecasts hold up?

Each forecast against real scans it never saw. Results land one at a time; a slot without its file says so.

held outLe Petit Prince, section 8

held out; first measured Oct 22-23; LPP is one of TRIBE v2's own evaluation sets. ~5 volunteers' worth (at least 2). Group level.

On a held-out audiobook, the forecast was worth about 5 real volunteers, and at least 2. It agreed with the classic sound test.

Each dot is one volunteer scan; a new story usually needs 12-15. Volunteer-equivalent 5.0, lower bound 2.58, 49 listeners.

What it had to beat: word rate and the sound envelope (beaten in 8 of 8 comparisons). In language areas it beat a simple word-rate model in 43 of 49 listeners.

Two numbers for the same 49 listeners: the held-out language result is 1.76 z, with word rate and sound partialled out of the forecast as the language gauge does (results/section8.json); the comparison with word rate and the sound envelope scores the forecast unpartialled, 1.65 z over the same 145 time points, so that every predictor is scored the same way (results/section8_baselines.json).

Le Petit Prince, section 8, held out: recomputed with code built during the event; first measured Oct 22-23. Baselines: rule committed after section 8 was first read (Oct 22-23) (exploratory); results/section8.json, results/section8_baselines.json.

passnotthefall: real listeners, intact versus sentences shuffled

With zero same-clip controls, stock TRIBE v2's language readout was stronger when the same healthy listeners heard a public story intact than when they heard its sentences shuffled (Narratives notthefall, 22 listeners, 9.1 minutes = 365 TRs per version; the order of their two runs is not public).

Each version also had its own TRIBE forecast, so a pass does not separate a change in the listeners' brains from a change in TRIBE's forecast, and the run order is not public, so it cannot be separated from an order or repeat-listening effect. Intact vs the coarser scramble (descriptive): HL 0.0215 [95% CI -0.0068, 0.0538], p = 0.0735, 21 listeners, 9.1 minutes = 365 TRs per version; did not reach p < 0.05.

Exploratory, same test, language areas: word rate alone p = 0.61, sound envelope alone p = 0.93 (22 listeners, 9.1 minutes = 365 TRs per version).
