StillHere Pilot

A researcher types the study they want to run. StillHere builds the stimulus conditions and their controls, Meta's Voice Transcribe checks them, and TRIBE v2 forecasts the healthy-adult brain response. Every forecast is shown next to real public scans.

Research tool for brain-imaging labs, built on Meta's TRIBE v2. Healthy volunteers, public data. Not a diagnosis or medical advice.

Passive listening tests can show that a brain still processes speech without asking the patient to do anything, which marks patients worth a full follow-up scan. But every new story needs its own 12-15 healthy volunteers scanned on it, in that language. StillHere needs none: Meta's TRIBE v2 stands in for them, and every reading is measured against the person's own resting brain.PREREG §7, allowed now, first sentence as replaced by D18

LPP section 8

Public Le Petit Prince listeners, no same-story volunteers; English numbers from held-out section 8 (recomputed with code built during the event; first measured Oct 22-23).

  • With zero matched volunteers, both readouts picked up English and French listeners as a group, and the language readout followed the real story over a word-shuffled one.public LPP, group level, 145 TRs = 4.8 minutes; English confirmed on held-out section 8; French from the development section (recomputed with code built during the event; first measured Oct 22-23)
  • On a story it had never seen, TRIBE alone did the work of about 5 healthy volunteers, and at least 2.the single volunteers line; held-out English section 8, LANGUAGE gauge: point estimate 5.0, lower 95% bound 2.6, floored to 2; development-section estimates are not quoted. M3 caveat applies. (recomputed with code built during the event; first measured Oct 22-23)
  • Add TRIBE to the few volunteers a hospital already has, and the test performs as if it had a few more.held-out English section 8 (49 listeners): LANGUAGE paired gain at k = 1 +0.79 [95% CI +0.58, +1.01] and at k = 2 +0.55 [+0.39, +0.72]; wrong-story check worst case 3 of 49 (6%) detections at k = 1, inside the 7% limit. M3 caveat applies. (recomputed with code built during the event; first measured Oct 22-23)
  • In English, TRIBE's language readout beat the classic word-level references we tested (frequency, syntax, and language-model surprisal) in healthy listeners (145 TRs, 4.8 minutes; the held-out English section, LPP English section 8, supplies the number used on stage).PREREG §7 (recomputed with code built during the event; first measured Oct 22-23)
  • On hearing, TRIBE agrees with the classic sound test, a built-in check.PREREG §7

Sedation case study

Frozen group statistics only (PREREG §6 bars, Gate 1): no per-volunteer value, no subject ID, no timecourse. Included per state: Awake 15, Light 12, Deep 12, Recovery 15. 145 TRs = 4.8 minutes per state.

  • Gate 1: no sedation stage line; the frozen If-it-fails claims apply (Deep effective N 12; 145 TRs = 4.8 minutes per state)
  • P7 (pipeline sanity, read first): bar met; FPN LOO-ISC Awake > Deep p = 0.0017, n = 12; Deep AUD LOO-ISC above its null p = 0.00073, n = 12 (brain-to-brain ISC, not TRIBE) (145 TRs = 4.8 minutes per state)
  • P1 (co-primary): bar not met, so the claim becomes: HEARING + ISC replication; "TRIBE-only LANGUAGE did not reach the bar" (LANGUAGE Awake > Deep in 9 of 12 volunteers (needed 10), p = 0.0034, Hodges-Lehmann median change +1.11 z [95% CI +0.43, +1.78]; 145 TRs = 4.8 minutes per state) Lag-sensitive (D24): its pass or fail differs at L +/- 1 s or at dL = 0.
  • P3 (co-primary): bar not met, so the claim becomes: One combined gauge, no split (LANGUAGE minus HEARING change from Awake to Deep, Hodges-Lehmann median -2.32 z [95% CI -3.56, -0.79], n = 12; 145 TRs = 4.8 minutes per state)
  • P8 (co-primary): bar met; Awake LANGUAGE with the real words > shuffled words in 10 of 15 volunteers, p = 0.018, Hodges-Lehmann +0.54 z [95% CI +0.12, +0.94] (145 TRs = 4.8 minutes per state) Lag-sensitive (D24): its pass or fail differs at L +/- 1 s or at dL = 0.
  • P2a: bar met under Holm; TRIBE HEARING in Deep above the rest null, p = 0.0024 (Holm threshold 0.01), n = 12 (145 TRs = 4.8 minutes per state) Lag-sensitive (D24): its pass or fail differs at L +/- 1 s or at dL = 0.
  • P2b: bar not met, so the claim becomes: No individual-level claim (TRIBE's individual detection rate against LOO-ISC's on the same runs, both gauges, n = 15; 145 TRs = 4.8 minutes per state)
  • P4: bar not met, so the claim becomes: "Replaces controls at group level only" (LANGUAGE AUC Awake vs Deep 0.80 [95% CI 0.60, 0.96], delta AUC vs LOO-ISC -0.06, n = 12; 145 TRs = 4.8 minutes per state)
  • P5 HEARING: bar met under Holm; N3 ratio 0.00, N4 ratio 0.21, p = 3.1e-05 (Holm threshold 0.0083), n = 15 (145 TRs = 4.8 minutes per state) Lag-sensitive (D24): its pass or fail differs at L +/- 1 s or at dL = 0.
  • P5 LANGUAGE: bar met under Holm; N3 ratio -0.01, N4 ratio -0.09, p = 0.015 (Holm threshold 0.017), n = 15 (145 TRs = 4.8 minutes per state) Lag-sensitive (D24): its pass or fail differs at L +/- 1 s or at dL = 0.
  • P6: bar met under Holm; LANGUAGE Recovery > Deep p = 0.01 (Holm threshold 0.013), median Recovery +0.81 z, median Awake +0.94 z, n = 12 (145 TRs = 4.8 minutes per state) Lag-sensitive (D24): its pass or fail differs at L +/- 1 s or at dL = 0.
  • Gate 1 timing: L set by D24; scored before the 9 PM ET decision-table label, recorded in D25.
